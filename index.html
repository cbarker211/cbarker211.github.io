Why it matters

An emerging, poorly-quantified source of emissions

As launch cadence accelerates and megaconstellations grow, rocket exhaust and re-entry burn-up are an increasingly significant and poorly understood source of atmospheric pollutants. This tracker makes the underlying data explorable, filterable and downloadable.
